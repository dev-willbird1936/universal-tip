# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People reading public online discussions and articles who want to find a creator's own published way to receive support. They are browsing another site, often in a compact browser panel.

## Product Purpose

Make it easier to discover public, attributable creator support information on demand, with sources and clear uncertainty. Discovery is not payment authorization. An isolated PayPal sandbox demonstration shows the payment technology without paying a discovered person.

## Brand Personality

Careful, clear, helpful. Explain evidence without implying certainty or pressuring the user to pay.

## Anti-references

Avoid intrusive overlays, fabricated verification badges, hidden data collection, cluttered dashboards and simulated live results.

## Design Principles

- Evidence and attribution precede conclusions.
- Initiate research only when the user chooses to.
- Keep the original page usable and the panel easy to scan.
- Explain unavailable services and unsafe matches plainly.
- Keep sandbox activity visibly separate from creator discovery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, live status announcements and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
